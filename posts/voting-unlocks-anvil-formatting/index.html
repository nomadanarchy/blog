Voting unlocks anvil formatting

Published 19th october 2026.

Voting has always helped the server show up on lists so new players can find it. It now also unlocks permanent anvil formatting codes.

There are still no kits, no free items and no economy rewards. The only thing voting gives is the ability to use formatting codes when renaming items in an anvil.

Unlocks

  • 5 total votes - Bold (&l)
  • 10 total votes - Italic (&o)
  • 20 total votes - Underline (&n)
  • 30 total votes - Strikethrough (&m)
  • 50 total votes - All color codes

Unlocks are permanent. Once you reach a threshold you keep the formatting even if you stop voting.

How to vote

Type /vote in game for the links, or open the vote page on the main site.

You can vote on as many lists as you want. Each successful vote counts toward your total.
